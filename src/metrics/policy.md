# Metric Policies

Metric standards are maintained by the Metrics Standardization Group (MSG) (link to contact information).

## How to get more information

The canonical source of truth for metric definitions and metadata is [Metric Hub](https://github.com/mozilla/metric-hub), a repository of metric, data source, and segment definitions that are reviewed before merging. See the [Metric Hub concepts page](https://docs.telemetry.mozilla.org/concepts/metric_hub.html) for more.

Supporting metric information can also be found on the [Metrics Inventory](https://mozilla-hub.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DATA/pages/620494911/Metrics+Inventory) page in Confluence (Mozilla LDAP required).
